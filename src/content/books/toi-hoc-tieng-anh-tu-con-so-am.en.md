Before picking up **"I Learned English From Below Zero"** (Tôi Học Tiếng Anh Từ Con Số Âm), I was the typical English learner: scared to speak, notebooks full of grammar, yet silent in conversation. I bought the book mainly out of curiosity: *"Can someone who started from even lower than me—from below zero—really succeed?"*.

Then, one evening in 2024, I picked it up and couldn't put it down. I read it straight through for 3 hours, completely absorbed, as if I had found a lifeboat in the middle of the ocean.

## A Story of Radical Empathy

What surprised me most was the author's authenticity. Mr. Nguyen The Anh doesn't lecture or boast about achievements; instead, he recounts his own raw, frustrating journey of learning English the wrong way for over 10 years.

Page after page, I saw myself: writing down notes obsessively, focused on grades, yet completely useless in real conversations. This shared struggle broke down the psychological barrier of "I have no talent" that I had built up for so long.

## The Key: Effortless English Method

The book doesn't teach exam tricks but focuses on the core mindset of the **Effortless English** method, which I had been following for a while:

1. **Learn like a child**: Listen – Understand – Speak first, then learn to Read – Write.
2. **Learn with your ears, not your eyes**: Instead of staring at text, listen to Mini Stories repeatedly until they sink into your subconscious.
3. **Stop translating**: Forget translating word-for-word from Vietnamese. Understand context and react naturally.

## A "Map" Rather Than a Text Book

My favorite part is the honesty: the author doesn't promise "fluency in 30 days." Instead, he offers a long-term strategy so that even if you start from "below zero," you can eventually reach the destination.

The book provides a specific roadmap, suggested materials, and how to build your own study plan. It feels more like a "journey diary + method map" than a dry textbook.

**Conclusion**: The greatest value this book gave me was **belief**. It empowered me to discard all my old, ineffective learning methods and start fresh—this time, in the right direction. If you are lost and starting from scratch, dedicate a quiet evening to read and reflect on this book.
